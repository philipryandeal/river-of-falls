# Stone 07: The Shore Stone

> "I do not know what I am, and I am still here."

The water whispers: can you stand on both halves?

This is the last stone before the fog. It holds for the walker who can carry the not-knowing without letting it dissolve the being-here, and without letting the being-here pretend to settle the not-knowing.

## The far shore

There is no prize. The far bank looks exactly like the near bank, carved all over with the falls of those who crossed before you.
